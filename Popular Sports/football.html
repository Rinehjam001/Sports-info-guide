Overview

Football revolves around one ball, which 2 teams of 11 compete for possession of, then try to get the ball in the opposing team's goal. These are known as "goals," only happening a few times each game, if at all. The team with the most goals at the end of 90 minutes, as well as any extra time due to penalties or injuries, is the winner.

Common Terms

  • Dribble:

    When a player kicks the ball repeatedly on the same path that they are moving.

  • Header:

    Relatively self-explanatory, this is when a player uses their head to hit the ball.

  • Throw-In:

    Following the ball going out of bounds off the long sides of the field, the team who did not touch the ball last will throw the ball back in bounds.

  • Free Kick:

    When the ball is hit out of bounds on either short side of the field by the team whose goal does not correspond to that end of the field, the other team’s goalie will kick the ball from either corner of the smaller rectangle next to the goal.

  • Corner Kick:

    The counterpart to free kicks, corner kicks occur when a team hits the ball out of bounds on their own short side of the field, giving the other team a kick from the corner on the side the ball went out on.

  • Yellow Card:

    Functions as a warning for the players or coaches when they commit an offense. Does not do anything on its own.

  • Red Card:

    Given out by referees when a player or coach commits an action that is “too far,” for lack of a better term. A red card ejects any player or coach given one out of a game. Can also be achieved by accumulating 2 yellow cards.

Overview

Two teams of 5 try to put a ball through a hoop as many times as possible in 48 minutes, split up into four 12-minute time periods, known as "quarters." There is a semicircle on the court around each net. Scoring within that line gains you 2 points, and scoring anywhere outside of it gains you 3. Instead of carrying the ball like many other ball sports, players have to "dribble," bouncing the ball on the floor as they move.

Common Terms

  • Dunk:

    When a player puts the ball directly in the hoop without it leaving their hands.

  • Layup:

    Although it is often used as a substitute for the term “freebie” in other contexts, in basketball, a layup is when a player tosses the ball into the hoop while they’re right next to it.

  • Paint:

    The paint refers to the differently colored area on the court in a rectangle near both hoops.

  • Foul:

    If a player makes physical contact with a player that is too disruptive, they are called for a “foul,” and the other team is rewarded the ball. If a team gets fouled 6 times in a quarter, or if a player is fouled while in the motion of shooting, they are rewarded with 2 free throws.

  • Handle:

    This refers to a player’s ability to dribble the ball.
